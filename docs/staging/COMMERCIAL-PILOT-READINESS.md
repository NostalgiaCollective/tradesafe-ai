3. **Review:** appoint qualified content and privacy reviewers; decide the pilot agreement, export custody, retention and offboarding. Prepared proposals are not approvals.
4. **Entry:** when domain/sender work is authorized again, establish a verified sender and nominate an unused controlled recipient for one signup test. Domain purchase and delivery remain deferred now. Preserve existing accounts/login/recovery.
